MediaPipe 21 Landmarks & ASL Sign Reference

The system tracks 21 coordinate landmarks on your hand in real-time:

Key Finger Tips:
  • Thumb: Landmark 4
  • Index: Landmark 8
  • Middle: Landmark 12
  • Ring: Landmark 16
  • Pinky: Landmark 20
Supported Gestures:
  • V: Index & Middle UP (Victory)
  • L: Index UP, Thumb OUT
  • B: 4 fingers UP together
  • A: Fist with thumb upright
  • YES: Thumbs UP
  • NO: Thumbs DOWN
  • HELLO: Open palm wave
  • THANK YOU: Thumb + Index + Pinky
